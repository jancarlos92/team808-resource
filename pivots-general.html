General Business Pivots

Transportation, manufacturing, field services, and multi-location businesses. Pick the situation you're in from the list and the card comes up.

The Six Moves

1 · BRIDGE

Use what they said or something true about their business.

2 · CONTEXT

Add an observation so the question doesn't feel random.

3 · OPTIONS

Offer two experiences. Easy to answer, never an interrogation.

4 · REACT

Acknowledge first. Positive, vague, or short: all useful.

5 · PIVOT

Industry, software, business cycle, or finance process.

6 · VALUE + CLOSE

Once they've opened the door: one value prop, then two days, morning or afternoon.
